# PureAmp

### Your music. Your player. Your freedom.

PureAmp is a free, open-source Magisk module that enables alternative music players within the HiBy R4's native Pure Audio Mode.

Originally developed to replace HiBy Music with Poweramp, PureAmp was born from curiosity, reverse engineering, and the belief that owning hardware should include the freedom to decide how it operates.

## Features

- Automatic Poweramp launch when entering Pure Audio Mode.
- Native Pure Audio Mode restrictions preserved.
- Adaptive boot recovery.
- Physical playback and volume controls supported.
- Normal Android Mode remains unaffected.
- Systemless and reversible through Magisk.
- Compatible with our HiBy R4 Android Auto modification.
- No firmware patches or SELinux policy modifications.
- No advertisements, subscriptions, or telemetry.

## Requirements

- HiBy R4.
- Root access through Magisk.
- Poweramp installed.
- Basic familiarity with Android modification and ADB.

## Rooting your HiBy R4

If your device is not already rooted, refer to our original project:

[HiBy R4 Android Auto — Rooting and Recovery Documentation](https://github.com/oslara/HiBy-R4-Android-Auto)

Installing the Android Auto module is not required.

## Installation

1. Download the PureAmp module ZIP from the Releases section, once published.
2. Switch your device to normal Android Mode.
3. Open Magisk.
4. Select Modules → Install from storage.
5. Select the PureAmp ZIP.
6. Reboot your device.
7. Switch to Pure Audio Mode.

## Choose your own player

Poweramp is our reference implementation, but it doesn't have to be yours.

PureAmp encourages experimentation. Users are welcome to inspect, modify, and adapt the source code to support their preferred music applications.

Compatibility with other players is not yet verified.
